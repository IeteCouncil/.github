## 🟩 SPAWN POINT

Welcome to the IETE Engineering World.

Every journey begins at a spawn point. You arrive here with nothing but curiosity — no map yet, no gear, no idea how big the world is. That's fine. That's how everyone starts.

**IETE** — the Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers — brings together people interested in electronics, telecommunications, information technology, and related engineering disciplines, with an emphasis on technical learning, professional development, collaboration, and innovation. This GitHub organization is where that mission gets hands-on: real projects, real code, real hardware, built in the open by the community itself.

Here, curiosity is your compass, engineering is your toolkit, and collaboration is your strongest resource.

## 🛠️ HOW TO BUILD WITH US

```
      🧭 EXPLORE
          ↓
       📜 QUEST
          ↓
       🍴 FORK
          ↓
       🔨 BUILD
          ↓
       🧪 TEST
          ↓
       🔍 REVIEW
          ↓
       🚀 PULL REQUEST
          ↓
       🌍 MERGE
          ↓
       ⭐ +XP
```

1. **Explore** — look around the biomes and mines above.
2. **Quest** — pick an issue, a roadmap project, or your own idea.
3. **Fork** — copy the repository into your own space.
4. **Build** — write the code, design the circuit, run the experiment.
5. **Test** — break it, fix it, break it again.
6. **Review** — ask someone to look at it with you.
7. **Pull request** — open it up for merging.
8. **Merge** — it's part of the world now, visible to everyone after you.

You do not need to be an expert to start any of these steps. You need to be willing to try.
